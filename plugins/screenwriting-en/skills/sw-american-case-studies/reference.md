## I. 《一夜风流》 (It Happened One Night): Three Motivation Shifts (minutes)

| Minute | Event | Motivation shift |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | Peter discovers Ellie's identity and decides to use the exclusive story to follow her all the way | The man's first |
| 29 | Ellie, threatened and tempted, agrees to share a room and go to New York, and the Walls of Jericho go up | The woman's first |
| 75 | Ellie confesses her feelings; Peter goes back to the newspaper office overnight for his fee and proposes (the central turning point) | Mutual transformation |
| — | Misunderstanding: Peter does not return in time; Ellie is thrown out of the room, asks her father for help, and agrees to marry King | |
| 94 | Peter goes to Ellie's house, meets her father, and asks for the $36.90 he spent on her — the father recognizes his decency and true love | The man's third |
| Ending | At the wedding, the father urges her to run away | The woman's third |

The father, Andrews, changes without logic; the writer portrays the aviator King as a treacherous little man, and Andrews opposes him from the start, which keeps the motivation consistent.

## II. 《双虎屠龙》 (The Man Who Shot Liberty Valance): Opposition Table

| Dimension | Tom (cowboy) | Ransom (lawyer) |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Lifts him easily | Is lifted |
| Dress | Cowboy hat and belt gun; later a black suit | Suit and tie; later a white apron |
| On guns | "There's a way to handle things out here" | "I don't need a gun, but I want him in jail" |
| Toward Sheriff Link | Teases and mocks him | Respects him |
| Rival in love | Hallie is cold to him | Hallie is attentive to him |

The meaning of the gun: learning to use a gun = overcoming an inner obstacle; losing the gun = failure (the near-sighted Schofield Kid and the poor shot Ned in 《不可饶恕》 (Unforgiven) — "the saddest thing there is for a cowboy"). The supporting character Link is gluttonous and cowardly and serves the comic passages; he is the director's attitude toward the people of the West.

## III. Wilder Dialogue Examples

**《热情似火》 (Some Like It Hot), the sleeping car**: Jerry's "I'm a girl, I'm a girl" self-hypnosis; Sugar climbs into his berth at midnight: "Thanks for saving my life — sisters are supposed to help each other," "If there's anything I can do for you," "There's plenty you can do for me"; "When I was little I used to share a bed with my sister, hide under the sheets, pretend we were lost in a cave and try to find a way out," "You're trembling all over," "How could I be?" "Your head is hot too," "Your feet are cold," "Let me warm them up"... "I'm a sick girl," "Not that kind of sick," "Let's have a whiskey" — looking for a paper cup, "Turn on the light," "No, I don't want anyone to know we're having a party," "I'll spill it," "Then spill it — the more you spill the more fun it is, maybe we can make a surprise party out of it," "What surprise?" "It's not time to tell you yet."

**《午夜》 (Midnight), the bar**: "They're beating up the man who called the taxi"; "Because he doesn't need a taxi"; "Then why did he call one?"; "Because I paid him five francs"; "I need forty francs a day, I earn forty francs a day, double on rainy days... If you want peace of mind, get yourself a taxi"; "No woman ever finds peace in a taxi; I'm looking for a limousine"; "They drive the same"; "It's better than the subway"; "I wish you'd throw the money in her face!"; "How can I throw a whole handful of money?"; "Anyone who wants something for nothing deserves what he gets"; "You can't just fall into a gold mine — you have to jump in"; "Now you're the one talking like a fool."

**《异国鸳鸯》 (Ninotchka), the traffic island**: "How long do we wait here"; "Until the policeman blows his whistle again"; "How long is the interval between whistles?"; "I never thought about it before... I've spent a lot of my life waiting for whistles"; "In other words, you don't know?"; "How long does it take a man to jump?"; "Last time I jumped I forgot to time it"; "I'm only interested in the shortest distance between two points. Do you have to flirt?"; "I don't have to, but it comes more naturally"; "I kissed that Polish cavalryman too — just before he died."

**《双重赔偿》 (Double Indemnity), Keyes**: "A claims adjuster is a surgeon, the desk is the operating table, the pencil is the scalpel and the chisel... he can be doctor, detective, policeman, judge, jury, and father confessor all in one"; "I picked you not because you're smart but because you're not as dumb as the rest of the company"; "No one has ever committed suicide by jumping off the back of a train going fifteen miles an hour"; "Closer than that"; "I love you, too."

## IV. 《米尔德丽德·皮尔斯》 (Mildred Pierce): Timeline of the Eight Drafts

| Date | Version | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Jan. 21, 1944 | Williamson outline, 28 pages | Three suggestions: change the statement to present tense; use flashbacks when telling the police; make Bert a suitable husband |
| Spring–summer 1944 | Turney's first and second versions | Realistic domestic melodrama; three mother-daughter confrontations; 147 pages, stopping at two-thirds of the novel; no film-noir style |
| — | Maltz's 62-page critique | Too episodic and biographical; Veda is not credible; cut Ray; the two men must be "charming failures"; "the film might better be called 'Courage'" |
| — | Gruen version | Baroque excess, the thinnest characters |
| — | Faulkner version | Gothic: black rings of mascara, poison, "a house built on sand," Lottie singing "Escape" |
| Oct.–Dec. 1944 | MacDougall's three versions | Present-tense murder + flashback struggle; first draft 163 pages; by Dec. 5, the first 84 pages were revised |
| — | Pearson revision | The office scene firing Monty was adopted |
| Dec. 16, 1944–Mar. 17, 1945 | Shooting period | As with 《卡萨布兰卡》 (Casablanca), most scenes were only settled one or two days before they were shot |

## VI. 《心灵捕手》 (Good Will Hunting): Deletions and Additions

| Scene in the source | Director's treatment | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| South Boston L Street grill bar (pp. 70–72, Will and his friends telling jokes) | Deleted; replaced by Will's first appearance reading a thick book in his room | Screens out what differs from the mainstream and highlights the genius |
| South Boston rooftop (73–74, Sean's exposition) | Moved to after Lambeau asks for Sean | "Triggers a POV shift or introduces a new character" |
| Chuckie's apartment / street (121–123, Chuckie with Skylar, his mother, the priest) | Deleted | Ignores the attention to reality; Sean matters more |
| Empty tram shot | Added | Expresses mood |
| Chuckie picks up Will (103–104) | Added several scenes | Gives the parking-lot monologue weight |
| "If we're discussing your life and who you are, then I'm fascinated" | Added one line | Merges two goals into one |
| Sean and Will's first meeting | Focus on Sean, Will's back to camera | Lowers identification with the protagonist and raises the supporting character |

Climactic value conflict: Lambeau — "I got where I am today because I was forced to, because I learned to force myself!" — Sean — "But he's not you!"

## VII. 《美国美人》 (American Beauty): DV POV Appearances

9:13, the kitchen conversation between Lester and Jane; 39:20, Ricky, through Angela's posing, films Jane's smile in the mirror; 54:05, filming the dead bird; 61:52, Jane and Ricky watch the videotape together; 69:07, Jane undresses in front of the window and offers her body to Ricky; 78:45–84:18, mutual filming (three screens). Cheerleading performance 13:49–19:01: objective → zoom → subjective (top light, high-speed photography, four zippers, rose petals) → back to objective, leaving an aftertaste. Three acts: numbness and awakening (to 36) / struggle (37–84) / transcendence (85–). Three reds: the roses, the door, the title.

## IX. Masterclass Films: Quick Node Reference

**《谍影重重》 (The Bourne Identity)**: page 6, the core question "Who are you?"; page 7, "Ivy League Ollie North"; page 10, the microfilm in his hip → drive; page 29, the embassy; page 45, he discovers he is an assassin; 65–67, the apartment shoot-out midpoint; page 75, a skilled driver; page 81, the low point; page 84, he changes his appearance; 93–95, unwilling to accept.
**《冒牌老爸》 (Big Daddy)**: page 1, "32 years old and still a child"; page 10, the failed proposal; page 15, "I can't lose her"; page 16, the kid appears; page 26, the "hearsay" mistake; page 29, Phil tells him to give up; page 41, the vow; page 44, the girlfriend and the reliable old man; page 45, "You're a good man"; page 61, he meets Layla; page 71, worries about the child's school.
**《疯狂动物城》 (Zootopia)**: opening, the little girl's stage play (herbivores and carnivores coexisting); minute 10, her first day at work, no one takes her seriously; minute 30, she is assigned the otter case; minute 46, the scratched car → the animals have gone savage; midpoint, the argument with the buffalo officer; the fox reveals his childhood secret.
**《美人鱼》 (The Mermaid)**: first minute, the sonar; minute 10, 珊珊 (Shan Shan) gives her phone number; minute 30, 刘先生 (Mr. Liu) calls him to the meeting, intending to kill him; 30–60, sea urchins / golf clubs; midpoint, Liu enters her family's home to kill him, "killing me won't solve your problem"; minute 70, searching the internet for mermaids.
**《银河护卫队》 (Guardians of the Galaxy)**: minute 15, Peter and Gamora each want to kill the other; minute 30, the villain wants to kill the heroine, and Peter persuades him not to.
**《美女与野兽》 (Beauty and the Beast)**: minute 45, the petal falls; the time-machine book goes back to see the Beast's mother die.
**《41 天》 (41 Days)**: pizzeria opening; 3-minute gunfight; minute 4, the same face transitions to backstory; minute 11, into the pipe; page 33, the first firm decision to escape (the cockroaches file around a corner and the shot lands on his face); midpoint, the police trace the phone → Junior is arrested; end of Act Two, the escape and the Gamates fight; the "bookend" ending.

## X. 《物质享受》 (Creature Comforts): Pros and Cons of the Suitcase Options

| Option | Pros | Cons / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plane tickets and passport | Immediately pushes forward (to Paris); the passport name/photo can take us back to earlier years | |
| Keys | Finding the lock is the next stop | |
| Corpse or body parts | Pushes to the climax extremely fast; the romantic moment turns into murder | "Creation swarming with sadists and perverts" |
| Photos / CDs | The owner must become real in the story; the CD must be played at the end | |
| Rhino horn / tiger penis | Smuggled treasures and scams | |
| Clothing (wedding dress / diving gear) | Delayed explanation (wedding album); at the end he puts it on and dives into the quarry | Explaining it through scattered dialogue is too clumsy |
| Condoms / fingernails | Directly connects to the heavy theme; DNA testing | |
| Drugs | | "Avoid at all costs" |
| Empty suitcase | The luggage itself is the theme (smuggling in the cart) | |
| Cannot be opened | Metaphor; opened by another character | |
| Twilight Zone method | Different every time it is opened (Herb is a writer making it up) | Must obey logic; no "it was all a dream" |

The author's version: expensive clothes, a tape recorder, a .45 pistol, a million dollars, a contract schedule, a manuscript of Creature Comforts missing forty pages. Working backward from the ending: prisoner writer — the Berkowitz law — pen name Peggy Turner — publisher exploitation — the impostor is silenced — the warden's accent — identity theme.
