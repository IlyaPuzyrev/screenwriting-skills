## I. McKee's Five-Step Scene Analysis Examples

### 《卡萨布兰卡》 (Casablanca): the linen stall (act climax)
- Conflict: Rick's desire is "to win Ilsa back"; Ilsa's is "to keep the past affair and continue her present life." Value = love; the opening is negative but tilting slightly positive.
- Beats: 1 approach / ignore; 2 protect / refuse intimacy ("You've been deceived"); 3 apologize (covering embarrassment with pedantic language) / refuse again; 4 find an excuse (wine) / a fourth refusal—an Arab vendor "sells" Rick to Ilsa by lowering the price; 5 put a foot in the door (a question whose answer is self-evident) / open a crack ("Yes"); 6 kneel and plead / demand more; 7 make her blame herself (the spare train ticket) / turn it back and make him blame himself; 8 say goodbye ("Remember, Paris isn't Casablanca") / reject the response; 9 accuse her of being a coward / accuse him of being a fool; 10 sexual provocation ("I'll be waiting for you above the café") / hide her face under the brim of his hat; 11 accuse her of being a whore / **crush hope with the news** ("Laszlo is my husband, and he was when I knew you in Paris"—a half-truth).
- Closing value: from a hopeful positive to a negative heavier than imagined. Each exchange is stronger than the last; the stakes rise and the will rises; the gap opens in the middle of beat 11; the turning point is the **revelation of the truth**.

### 《犹在镜中》 (Through a Glass Darkly): the attic scene (Bergman)
- Karin (fantasy-type schizophrenic) drives the scene; her desire is "to see God and have the men meet Him together"; the opposition is her husband Martin + her own subconscious. Opening value: hope.
- 20 beats: plan to escape / cover guilt → flee toward God / help her → pray / hurry to find her → prepare for the apparition / observe her madness → stop her / protect faith → deny God / defend → order her to leave / retreat → pull her into the ritual / surrender (kneel) → caress / break free → pray intently / **"God" announces His arrival** → appear / welcome → **attack / break away** → attempted rape / resist → flee / block → sedative / surrender → warn Minus / listen quietly—"the door opened, and God was a spider." The wardrobe door opening by itself + the helicopter landing = meaningful coincidence fused into a center of violent emotion. The turning point revolves around **action**, not revelation.

### 《克莱默夫妇》 (Kramer vs. Kramer): French toast
- Three values (confidence, the son's trust, the family's survival) turn simultaneously on three levels (inner doubt / the son crying / the alien world outside the kitchen) = a complex scene.

### 《唐人街》 (Chinatown): Act II climax, beat by beat (a demonstration of subjective point of view)
Gittes rehearses "I must stay calm" → sees the house and gets angry (the gap) → bangs on the door → "Wait a minute" → curses in Cantonese → Evelyn: "Thank God it's Jake" → "Where is that girl?" → silver cigarette case / lighter / glasses wrapped in a handkerchief → "That's where he drowned" → grabs her and lets go → "She's my sister / my daughter," the slap cycle → "She's my sister and my daughter" → "Did he rape you?" a shake of the head (the key rewrite: cut the explanation of the father's breakdown, keep Cross cruel, and test Gittes's love) → "Those aren't Hollis's glasses; he doesn't wear bifocals" (the gap closes) → 1712 Alameda Street. Step outside the subjective view and ask, "What is the opposite of that reaction?"

## II. Field: Sequence and Scene Examples

- 《网络》 (Network), the "I'm mad as hell" sequence: Beale gets soaked in the rain entering the building → control room → the speech "I'm a human being, goddammit!" → Diana checks the affiliates: "Is anybody shouting?" → Max's daughter opens the window and hears the whole city shouting → "We can't control this anymore."
- The "homecoming" sequence: fix the idea first, then fill in the content (the military adviser on the plane / the family getting up / cross-cutting / the military band at the airport / the reunion).
- 《银带》 (Silver Streak), the compartment scene: a tape recorder, champagne, "if you treated azaleas like nasturtiums," a corpse hanging outside the window—also Plot Point I.
- Contrast: 《意大利式离婚》 (Divorce Italian Style)—the Church forbids divorce → find a lover for the wife → kill her with impunity; Neil Simon's "conflicting wills" strike sparks.

## III. Walter: Location Replacement Exercise

| Original scene | Alternative | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| The inspector confronts his superior in an office | A swimming pool: the superior swims laps on doctor's orders, the inspector shouts from the poolside, chlorinated water splashes onto six-hundred-dollar crocodile shoes | Status humiliation vs. patient maneuvering; an ordinary cop vs. someone who can afford six-hundred-dollar shoes |
| A radical student meets his father in a restaurant | A car wash: the daughter, in work clothes, wipes the windshield | The audience does not doze off |
| A man and a woman meet at a water cooler | Changing a tire in a parking lot: "Is the women's movement just so a sister can break her nails, tear her stockings, and loosen the lug nuts?" | Conflict performed through action (jack, hubcap, spare tire) |
| At the brothers' restaurant, a female customer orders two main courses | The younger brother, the chef, refuses to make them | A metaphor for the conflict between commerce and art |
| A phone call | 《大西洋城》 (Atlantic City): in a phone booth, pretending to dial, he finds the package above the phone; 《金色年代》 (My Favorite Year): background clamor, the aunt puts on a wedding dress; 《铁钩船长》 (Hook): the phone is thrown into the snow | The phone becomes the center of action |

Walter's annotation marks: **$?** (is this line worth paying for); **3 Strikes** (purpose? value? best way?—in 《狮子王》 (The Lion King) the wildebeest stampede accomplishes three things at once); **Mood spelled backward is Doom** (slathering on mood cannot replace story); **Notnot** (do not write "no answer," "did nothing"); **Too Straight-Line** (cause followed quickly by effect = a straight line; go for checkerboard jumps); **Conk** (concrete); **Ess. Det. Only / SIFYN** (save it for your novel).

## IV. 陆军 (Lu Jun): Models of Suspense and Delay

### 《傻女婿卖布》 (The Foolish Son-in-Law Sells Cloth): Seven Suspense Hooks
Is the customer's story true? → Can the foolish son-in-law find it? → If he cuts the conversation short and cannot get the money, what then? → What does 万先生 (Mr. Wan) mean by sending a bamboo tube? → Why pack a bundle and go back to her parents' home? → Can they be reunited within seven days? → "The first wife is best": the change of heart.

### 《小酒店的一夜》 (One Night at the Tavern): Delay
史尼格 (Shnigg) panics and returns to the room, desperately begging the "master" to take back the ruby—"I don't want my share of the ruby now" "Nonsense, Shnigg" "My share is yours, master" "Are you going to inform on us?" "No, no, I just don't want the ruby" "Did you see the police?" "I swear I saw something I didn't like" "What is it you didn't like?" (eyes full of tears) "Ah, master, master, take it back"—after whetting the appetite, a cold, iron-hard footstep; the blind idol gropes for the ruby, picks it up, and sets it into its forehead; then the delay continues: the idol exits, but its voice calls out names one after another, and the robbers go out one after another.

### 《莱欧尼达先生遇见"反动派"的时候》 (When Mr. Leonidas Met the "Reactionaries"): Five Layers
He talks revolution at length to his wife → ominous action and sound → persuades his wife that there is no revolution → gunshots, newspapers, flight, packing, hurried knocking → opens the door to a total misunderstanding (a group of drunks).

### 《寿堂》 (The Birthday Hall): Two Approaches
| Keep the secret | Hand over the key |
|---|---|
| 焦、孟 (Jiao and Meng) enter in mourning clothes and report the bad news; the whole hall is shocked—the momentary effect of "surprise" | The audience, 穆桂英 (Mu Guiying), and 柴郡主 (Princess Chai) already know before the birthday banquet; forcing themselves to look happy, they ask Jiao and Meng to take off their mourning clothes and join the celebration—the charm of "interest"; 太君 (Tàijūn — the Grand Dowager) stands firm and raises her cup with tears in her eyes |

## VI. Lu Jun: Models for the Three Criteria of Setting

- **Theme**: 《我们要活下去》 (We Must Live): a small niche beside a tunnel where only two people can stand—a train comes and someone must die; Clyde waves at the train and it actually stops (a strike along the line).
- **Character**: 《三鞭子》 (Three Whips): a mud pit halfway up the mountain with three layers of background (the guerrilla base once sank a Japanese car and an Eighth Route Army gun carriage / cadres going to the countryside "eating at scattered households" / inconvenient transport, foreign businessmen saying "bye-bye") → Uncle Zhao (赵大爷) vents → wins face → opens his heart.
- **Conflict**: 《电梯》 (The Elevator): trapped in an elevator needing to "relieve themselves"—an aluminum pot and a black trench coat as a screen; 《旋转餐厅》 (The Revolving Restaurant): a picnic on the scaffolding overlooking Shanghai; 《张古董借妻》 (Zhang Gudong Lends His Wife): Moon City and the Yue family's house alternate monologues line by line; 《姐妹易嫁》 (Sisters Exchange Marriages): three spaces, a mirror dropped upstairs, dust falling into the wine glass downstairs.
- **Software**: 《渡娘桥》 (Duniang Bridge): a stone bridge + the folk song "A carrying pole, sharp at both ends" + the story of 秦娘 (Qin Niang) breaking the ice to cross the river; 《第七棵是玉兰树》 (The Seventh Is a Magnolia): the theme song three times, the origin of Love Plaza, the tree planter's "proudest day was the year I was twenty and joined the Communist Youth League."

## VII. Henson: Action Paragraphs and Transition Standards

- Action paragraphs read like a list; first time, season, and weather to convey atmosphere, then place and important physical features; write only what the camera can photograph and the actor can play; passive voice is a curse ("Joe is led by Paul" → "Paul leads Joe"); cut adjectives and adverbs; "she stands there thinking" cannot be filmed.
- Bases for a scene change: place/time, adding or removing characters, changing the dramatic objective; double-space between the last line of dialogue and the next slug line.
- Sound effects are bold only in action paragraphs, and written only when the source is unseen (thunder, a phone ringing, a dog barking off-screen).
- Bad version vs. good version, 《新鲜的奶酪》 (Fresh Cheese): at the UN, "for the past hour the delegates have been listening to Dash sound off" (backstory, past tense, impression from someone else's description) → a moon base collecting samples → a survival pod, a motorcycle jacket, Mozart, a switchblade cutting a slice, "My God, it's cheese!" → the UN shakes the cheese; the ambassador picks up the phone, "In ten minutes, bring grenades and rocket launchers."
- The officialese exercise: for two weeks, keep every sentence to ten words or fewer; cut conjunctions, subordinate clauses, adjectives, and adverbs; keep transitive verbs; "Pam folds clothes in the laundry room. The phone rings. Pam runs quickly to the kitchen and picks up the receiver. No one answers. Pam hangs up."

## VIII. Hicks: Scene Narrative Models

- 《小木屋》 (The Cabin): Mattie smells a stench, climbs out the window to look for "the dead thing"; Congressman George follows miserably with a wet towel around his face; Mattie discovers the raccoon is not dead and wants to be its new mother; "Take the money back, just carry me and it back to the city"—"I came all the way out here and all I got was a raccoon!" (leave before the conflict is fully resolved).
- Counterexample, 《旅馆房间》 (The Hotel Room): ceiling, Laura Ashley sheets, antique cabinet, remote control, the sound of shaving in the bathroom, "It's nice here, don't you think" "Did you just say something?"—no information, the plot does not move.
- 《灯塔楼梯》 (The Lighthouse Staircase): footsteps spiral upward, abnormal eyes in the shadows, Martha crouches ferociously, George jumps out the roof exit, Martha lunges into his arms, her fingers sink into his flesh, pushing toward the railing, body heat fogs the glass—put it in a visually interesting environment and something happens at once.
- Phone example: George answers the phone / Martha (V.O.) "You great big bastard!" / cross-cutting—without trespassing on the director's decisions.

## IX. 梅峰 (Mei Feng): The Flow of Value in the Restaurant Scene of 《双虎屠龙》 (The Man Who Shot Liberty Valance)

Ransom cites the law code → Link refuses (the Western code prevails) → Hallie wants to learn to read, her immigrant parents laugh (civilization gains a positive charge) → Tom changes into a black suit, "if you hang out a shingle you have to protect it with a gun" → Liberty trips Ransom, "the new waitress" (the Western code negative: violence does evil) → Tom's steady voice + the black partner in the kitchen raising a gun (only violence can subdue violence) → Ransom picks up the steak and slaps it onto Peabody's table, interrupting the confrontation → Liberty throws a bottle and fires wildly (if it stopped at paying the money and leaving, no information would be lost; this extra beat highlights that violence harms far more than it protects) → "a respectable scene started over gravy and potatoes" → "You can kill him or he can kill you over a steak not worth a penny!" → enter the kitchen (the restaurant = the dangerous West, the kitchen = solidarity and warmth). Multiple value pairs shift within one scene; every scene in which the cactus rose appears is a scene where a key value is at stake.
