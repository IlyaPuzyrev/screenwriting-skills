## I. Kakei Masaya's 《美女罐头》 (Canned Beauties), First Draft Plot Outline (2005.01.17)

Yuta (雄太), 23, a freelancer, is going to a supermarket for a job interview tomorrow; his live-in girlfriend Haruko (春子), before leaving on a business trip, wraps herself in the futon, holds him tight, and cries, and they promise to go to the zoo when she returns; as he sees her off, a middle-aged man in a tracksuit, Tomioka (富冈), moves in next door, and several young beautiful women help him move (**qi: meeting a beauty**); at the interview he forgets his résumé; at the supermarket he sees Tomioka again, buying a huge amount of food and women's underwear, accompanied by four or five beauties; late at night, a bingo party and "moaning" next door — his curiosity grows; in the morning a legion of more than 30 beauties greets him in unison like kept pets; while Tomioka is out, he sneaks in, finds a pink "beauty can," and steals a box; a DVD comes with it, "How to Make a Beauty Can"; 30 minutes later in the bathroom, "Saki" (沙纪) is born (**plot point: life with a beauty begins**); Saki has implanted memories; that night he makes a sexual advance, is refused, and she runs away in shock, falls asleep sitting in front of the supermarket; he apologizes, takes her hand, and they go home — "Saki is an extremely ordinary woman"; breakfast is terrible; she has a class to attend — "she is a university student (a character setting) — little by little, he discovers the details of her setting"; the DVD's main menu has "Saki's settings," and she comes back halfway through; on the third day they go to the zoo on a date, and he feels "human beings are selfish after all"; that night he finds "Best before 05.04.10" printed on her lower back (**zhuan: use a time limit to accelerate the story**); for three days he does not dare touch her; on a rainy night he goes to the interview again, and Saki, alone, sees the settings DVD and falls into a daze; Yuta comes home, sees that the DVD has been watched, and runs out; Saki stands in the rain in front of the supermarket, and Yuta walks up to her but cannot say anything; then Haruko returns from her trip and looks at the calendar — today is 05.04.08; she takes a "blue-wrapped can" from the kitchen and remembers the night before her trip — Yuta's exposed lower back also bears "Best before 05.04.08!"; in the rain in front of the supermarket, Saki's eyes reflect Yuta, and a clock can be heard counting down. The end.

**Method note**: After coming up with the big reversal that "the man is also a robot," trace backward to add setups and add an explanation at the beginning — "the theater kid's dream is shattered" — to inflate the plot: "a feature film should be made this way"; the theater kid was derived backward from the premise (you need someone who will not doubt being drawn into one thing after another; the actor has a shadow of a puppet about him); the beauty "belongs neither to men nor to women" → a robot; when adapting it into a 20-minute 《世界奇妙物语》 (Tales of the Unusual) episode, he cut the breakup line, changed the protagonist to a young freelancer, changed the ending to the sea in the rain (more cruel), and traced backward to add a seaside date.

## II. Kakei Masaya's 《人生补时》 (Loss: Time: Life), "Two People at the Same Time" Episode — Plot Outline

森保甫 (Morimoto Hajime), 27, a rookie manga artist; 康子 (Yasuko), 27, an office worker (married, dissatisfied with her marriage). Yasuko buys the magazine carrying Hajime's manga and buys a gift; Hajime changes their appointment because he has to deliver pages in the morning; on the train, a pair of elementary-school children playing a mobile-phone game triggers a memory (going with Yasuko to buy "Dragon Quest") — at 9:15 a.m. the train derails, everything around them stops, football referees appear in front of them and injury time begins, the electronic board reads "3 hours," and the live commentary says "the young man chasing his dream seems to have rarely wasted his life" (**qi**); in the residential district, countless referees in yellow uniforms run along the tracks; he calls Yasuko but cannot get through; he goes to Yasuko's family home, and her mother says Yasuko got married last year; Yasuko comes running — four referees are behind her too (**cheng**: she took the same train after being 15 minutes late because she had gone to get the gift); they talk on park swings and visit their old school, "I married because Hajime was so dense"; memories: the love letter returned with its typos corrected, passing each other in the middle-school corridor, hiding in the high-school parking lot; the gift is a watch, its face cracked and stopped at "9:15" (**the final setup**); Hajime has "15 minutes" left, Yasuko has "1 hour 15 minutes" — their injury times differ (**zhuan**); the two return to the train, Hajime has "1 minute" left, and Yasuko suddenly has an idea — "Let me take that manga to the publisher for you!" "A manga artist whose only hit is a posthumous work is so cool" — she snatches the manuscript, "Yasuko... I have always liked you!" "You are saying it too late!" the whistle sounds; Yasuko runs desperately to the publisher, finds that the "typos" in several speech balloons have been corrected one by one with an eraser and pencil, and reads the whole thing through in tears; the editor mistakes her for Hajime's wife — "I have been his assistant for years," she says with a smile, and leaves. The end.

**Method note**: "If there were injury time before death, and it were commented on like a live football broadcast" — "if" plus "and"; the inspiration came from a painting (a bullet advancing slowly) and "people see in slow motion just before death"; "a man and a woman die in the same accident at the same time, and this is only learned later" was conceived as the "Answer Movie" to 《美女罐头》 (Canned Beauties); in the finished work, the "cheng" is placed in the second half as a painful turn.

## III. Kakei Masaya's 《恋爱部屋》 (Love Room — a love-room layout): Concept Points

"If you swap rooms, and moreover the lover who appears in your new life is the previous owner's lover"; 结衣 (Yui), an art-college student aiming to become a chair designer (so that she can have something in common with the architect 隆 (Takashi)), and 热子 (Atsuko), a lighting designer, swap rooms; a professional-wrestler moving crew (a real detail learned by chance from both a newspaper and a magazine at the same time); setup objects: men's shorts on the floor, a masked-wrestler design on a phone strap (the same on both women), a cup Takashi bought, ticket stubs; the trigger for learning about the swap: during a live TV broadcast of a fire, Atsuko sees Yui walk into the 202 room where she used to live; the ending was decided from the start — "move again and start a new life again." The producer's theme was "Francfranc's 15th anniversary, Tokyo, furniture" → the moving scene naturally captured furniture.

## IV. Miki Satoshi's 《速成沼泽》 (Instant Swamp): Working File (Tree Software)

- **Rough outline 1** (41 numbered small gags): a seemingly ordinary woman shows an unexpected side → kleptomania? / her life slowly goes downhill / debts keep increasing / she finds a cute nail that no one understands / should she join a new religion? / she believes only what she has seen with her own eyes / if she collects 27 ten-yen coins from Showa 53, something will happen / she sells only one tiny lucky cat all day / she quits her job / she starts running an antique shop / many things go wrong because a swamp is haunting her — she once threw a friend's belongings into the swamp and brought a curse on herself / she is gullible and also lies / when she is almost at her wit's end, a suspicious old man helps her (he is actually a good person) / a kappa appears / she rescues a doll thrown into the swamp / she gets one million yen / the old man wants to buy back the village warehouse / the mud is bought / but the mud is not mud — it is dried swamp → how should this be revealed? Explaining it directly is too boring — chocolate milk / the swamp used to be called the master's swamp, and the warehouse is what sealed it — that's it! Instant Swamp! / a dragon flies into the sky and the sky clears / two things combine into one.
- **Plot structure 1** (elements of the finished draft): my life's constant bad luck is blamed on a curse; my morning begins with a glass of chocolate milk; making espresso with instant coffee; quitting the job; the antique shop does not go well; the old man appears; "fermentation" (hakkō) and "unhappiness" (hakkō) sound the same in Japanese; she often searches for ten-yen coins from the year she was born; she tells a punk rock singer, "You guys are great, but you haven't settled on a track order!"; the warehouse is full of mud, and she is disappointed; a dragon flies from the swamp into the sky; you are really bad at holding an umbrella; a strange machine makes a cat's meow when you press it; the Black Cat shop sells only black antiques; the reason the old man became a swindler; what is called lifting the seal is just opening the lid of a brand-new "Milo"; turn on the tap!
- **Opening monologue** (excerpt): "Today, too, chocolate milk. ... Run, run — the hero of an independent film keeps running; people who say 'all you have to do in life is run' are rare in reality. ... One spoonful of happiness and ten spoonfuls of 'Milo,' plus 35 cc of milk, and even crying feels happy. Stir it once, and chocolate milk is made. ... Whether something happens in the world or nothing happens, my morning begins with chocolate milk."
- **Ending of Script 2 vs. Script 9**: Script 9 added 花芽 (Haname)'s speech on a cherry picker: "Listen to me — nothing in this world is a big deal; the time we spend laughing far exceeds the time we spend crying; we see things we never believed in, but after one sleep we forget them all. Anyway! Turn on the tap! Let it wash away your lies, greed, and vanity!" — "I put all the parts of myself I find embarrassing into it."
- **Structure**: the "temporary family" (someone goes to a completely different environment in order to grow, then returns); a person who denies the illusory finally encounters something uncanny; the sidekick "Gus" is built from an outward contrast.

## V. Kosawa Ryota's 《怪胎刑警》 (Gonzo: Legendary Detective), Episode 1 Framework

At the very top: 黑木 (Kuroki)'s dream (inner melancholy and trauma) → his present image, forced to return to work at the police station → the entrance of the heroine 鹤 (Tsuruko), a rookie police officer → a friend who holds the key to the case loses a violin → "蓬" (Yomogi) was the perpetrator of a certain case and later changed his name to "Nicholas" — "what is written in the framework is arranged in the order in which it must be presented to everyone in advance"; in the exact center, a scene in three parts shows three intercut shots. The protagonist Kuroki: once a famous figure in Investigation Division One, now mentally ill; lead actor 内野圣阳 (Uchino Masaaki) asked for "more flexibility" → the contrast between a flippant exterior and a lost inner self. The producer on the first draft of 《外事警察》 (Gaiji Keisatsu): "The content is too easy to understand... it doesn't matter if everyone doesn't understand it" — a challenge to make a "deformed script."

## VI. Omiya Ellie's 《木下部长和我》 (Director Kinoshita and Me): 企画書 (kikakusho — the series proposal) (2009.10.14)

1. **Story synopsis**: At the major advertising agency Marumaru Communications, where results and efficiency rule, the new department head 木下幸之助 (Kinoshita Kōnosuke) is often late and leaves early, "just going through the motions" — why was he made a department head? The team members react in different ways (oppose / ignore / give up / transfer out), and because their annual salary depends on departmental sales, they have no choice but to focus on sales; Kinoshita's seemingly willful actions become the occasion for cleverly completing assignments, and even when he fails he wins the company a bigger benefit; set against him is the 君岛 (Kimijima) department (elites), which colludes with the HR and inspection departments to obstruct him time and again — the battle between these two utterly opposite teams is the most exciting spectacle.
2. **Characters**: Director Kinoshita (played by 板尾创路, Itao Itsuji) — catchphrases "Time to go home," "Either one is fine," "Anything is fine"; motto "It's nothing, I won't die"; 仆元 (Bokumoto), played by 池田一真 (Ikeda Kazuma) — coolly observes and analyzes the environment from the perspective of a contemporary young person and delivers a report to the audience; he is completely incompatible with his fellow recruit 相川雅哉 (Aikawa Masaya).
3. **Synopses of the first two episodes + titles for all 12 episodes**.
4. **Planning purpose and selling points**: not a corporate drama in the usual sense, but "an accessible human drama that everyone living in society can relate to"; the humor of each person in Kinoshita's department "suddenly strengthens the sense of reality and strikes right at the heart"; "the truly important thing is for the essence of things to be discovered"; nostalgic like 《寺内贯太郎一家》 (Terauchi Kantarō's Family), and revealing a literary-fiction flavor within a human comedy like 《我是猫》 (I Am a Cat).

**Omiya's method**: clarify the commission's requirements → decide the theme yourself ("the computer is a friend" / "a forward-moving hiatus" / "cheer up the office worker") → fix the color tone and onomatopoeia → arrange characters like building a flower bed or preparing hot-pot ingredients → dialogue comes from inner conversation; "the characters move in my head, and all I have to do is record them"; "if you can write sentences, you can write a script"; when the crew says something is impossible, ask instead "how could it be done?" (a 70-minute long take shot in two days).

## VII. Fukuda Yuichi's 《33 分侦探》 (33 Minutes Detective): Fixed Form

A case occurs → Inspector 大田原 (Otagawara) comes to the detective agency (there is always a strange visitor talking to 里佳子 (Rikako)) → everyone rushes to the scene, and 鞍马六郎 (Kurama Rokurō) says, "Let me use the full 33 minutes to solve this case" → he catches the initial suspect and delivers an unreasonable deduction → **the location-change shot** (the Rainbow Bridge as a portable shrine, and no one questions it) → "I don't want to believe that people would kill this easily." Tsukkomi assignment: in the first draft Otagawara was the tsukkomi and Rikako was the natural boke → the producer's wife said "Isn't it the other way around?" → Otagawara, who believes in the detective, became the "big boke," and Rikako cut in with "That's not how it is." It came from two pieces of material: "you can beat an opponent in 30 minutes, so ○○man is stronger" + つかこうへい (Tsuka Kohei)'s 《热海杀人事件》 (The Atami Murder Case).

## VIII. Yukisada Isao's 《奢侈的骨》 (Luxurious Bone): A Prose-Poem Plot Outline

"Three goldfish are kept in a blender; three people are peering at the goldfish, two women and one man; they exchange incomprehensible looks, and there is a hidden secret... sometimes the three of them sleep huddled together, and one of the women stands up and stares motionless at the other two sleeping faces, then turns on the switch of the blender containing the goldfish" — 永濑正敏 (Nagase Masatoshi): "This is so interesting!"; 10 million yen, 8 million for production and 2 million for promotion, and the founding of the company Mouchette. The real source: a newlywed friend kept goldfish in a blender at home, "who did he go there with, again...?"

## IX. Takahashi Izumi's 《清晨之汤》 (One Morning, Soup): Two Versions of the Final Scene

- **The first version**: 北川 (Kitagawa) rides his bicycle to work through blooming cherry blossoms while 志津 (Shizu) sees him off — "Remember to bring it — riding a bike is fast" "I'll try — that's settled, bye" — and he still wears the rosary bracelet on his wrist (no one knows whether he has left the religion).
- **The finished version**: on the morning of the two's parting, Shizu's last line is the single word "other people"; the sound of chewing pickles echoes as the stage darkens — "only by fully acknowledging the 'other' and parting can each move on to the next step"; "I could not help crying when I wrote this ending."
- Method: give the actors sheets of paper with character settings ("a religion story suddenly begins," "going to a seminar") and have them improvise; write the script according to their reactions on the spot, but more than 90% of the dialogue is written by the screenwriter; the conflict over the bankbook is "real" — 并木 (Namiki) hyperventilates and collapses.

## X. Yokohama Satoko's 《奇迹爱情物语》 (Ultra Miracle Love Story): Two Initial Plots

"阳人 (Yōto) bathes in pesticide, dies, is reborn, dies again, and finally has his brains eaten by a bear" / "町子 (Machiko) is a kindergarten teacher; after meeting Yōto she falls in love, and while witnessing the changes in Yōto after he bathes in pesticide she lives with him, and in the end she goes into the forest with the kindergarten children" — combining the two plots and filling in what lies between them was "extremely hard work." Keywords: children and animals (living without a purpose), evolution, ADHD, pesticide (drugs change only the brain; the world around does not change), a person with no head, "God," the bear encounter placed in the final shot. Dialogue principles: keep ambiguity, value sound, say only what happened.

## XI. Uchida Kenji's Tools

- **Résumé**: how many siblings, which university/high school/middle school he graduated from, why he came to Tokyo and how many years he has been there, hobbies, his attitude toward women.
- **Diary**: write a three-day diary in the protagonist's voice for the three days of the story → "so that is what he was thinking then" → work backward to the setup (fool the other person and then run away → plant the prop earlier).
- **Framework**: four frames plus ideas → list shots and dialogue item by item → revise repeatedly.
- **Schedule**: for a story with many characters in one day, draw a timeline to the left of each name to guarantee physical consistency (it takes more time to ride the train from here to there).
- **The "unbeautiful" coincidence**: a dead phone battery is not beautiful; the phone having vanished earlier in an accident is beautiful; the audience does not allow coincidence at the crucial moment.
- **Return to what you like most**: "Please make the AV that excites you most" — do not change it to what everyone likes; an original script does not need to include shots you hate (a strip club).

## XII. Arai Haruhiko's 《W 的悲剧》 (The Tragedy of W): Adaptation Points

The original is a locked-room murder in a snow-mountain villa, "too boring to handle," and 角川春树 (Kadokawa Haruki) said, "As long as you keep the title, change whatever you like"; after a month he thought of using the original as a play-within-a-play and wrapping it around the theme of "a bit player chasing a dream"; he interviewed girls at a theater company's training class for a month; references were 《彗星美人》 (All About Eve) and 《日落大道》 (Sunset Boulevard); W = the initial of the original protagonist / WOMEN / double V; at the press conference the girl willingly becomes a scapegoat in exchange for a role — "If I don't take money from him, you will all believe that we are truly in love, won't you"; "she is lying, but her tears are real"; the creative process: one month of field research, one month for the synopsis, then set it aside to drink and play, and two weeks before the deadline go through the whole thing. 《日本的天空下》 (This Country's Sky): "When you write about eating, always write what dishes were eaten; when you write about sex, write everything from undressing to the end" — "daily life within an extraordinary event."
