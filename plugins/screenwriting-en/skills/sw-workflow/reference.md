## 2. Stage 2 Worksheet: BS2 Page-Number Conversion

Take the proportions based on 110 pages, multiply them by the target page count, and round:

| Beat | Proportion | 100 pages | 90 pages | 30-page short film |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opening image | 1% | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Theme stated | 5% | 5 | 4 | 2 |
| Catalyst | 11% | 11–12 | 10 | 3 |
| Break into two | 23% | 23–25 | 21 | 7 |
| B story | 27% | 27 | 25 | 8 |
| Midpoint | 50% | 50 | 45 | 15 |
| All is lost | 68% | 68–70 | 61 | 20 |
| Dark night of the soul | 68–77% | 70–77 | 61–69 | 20–23 |
| Break into three | 77% | 77–79 | 69 | 23 |
| Final image | 100% | 100 | 90 | 30 |

Filling format: `beat | page | one-sentence content`. After filling it in, check: setup ≤ 25%; catalyst ≤ 20 pages; the last act is the shortest.

## 3. Stage 4 Worksheet: Step-Outline Row Format

```
scene number | INT/EXT · location · DAY/NIGHT | one sentence (who does what, and the result differs from expectation) | value start → end | structural position | +/- | ><
```

- Write the value as the positive or negative pole of a binary pair: `obedience (+) → transgression (-)`, `trust (-) → trust (+)`. Delete any scene whose start and end values are the same.
- `><` states "who wants what, who blocks it, who wins." Write only one conflict per scene.
- For structural position, use a BS2 beat name or an act/sequence number; when checking against `sw-truby-anatomy`, you may also mark the 22-step name; projects with multiple storylines add the line number at the end of the row.
- Divide the 40 cards into four rows: 1–25% / 25–50% / 50–75% / 75–100%; if act three has fewer than 6 cards, go back and check the B story and the six things that need fixing.

## 4. Stage 6 Worksheet: Sources for the Revision-Target Table

Run them in order, and copy each failed item into one line of the revision-target table:

1. `sw-story-structure`, Section 10, structure diagnostic, 13 questions
2. `sw-character-conflict`, Section 8, character diagnostic, 14 questions
3. `sw-scene-craft`, Section 9, scene diagnostic, 13 questions
4. `sw-dialogue`, Section 9, dialogue diagnostic, 14 questions
5. `sw-format-adaptation`, Section 6, format diagnostic (divided into four groups by convention: A, 12 general questions, run for all three conventions; B, 7 Hollywood-style questions / C, 8 questions for the Chinese numbered-scene format / D, 6 questions for the Japanese format — run only the one you use)
6. `sw-truby-anatomy`, Section 10, the ten questions for a sagging middle (when act two scatters into isolated events, or when "every scene is right but they do not connect")
7. `sw-genre-anatomy`, Section 17, genre diagnostic, 12 questions (when a genre draft is "competent but bland," lacks the beats that genre's audience expects, or mixed genres fight one another)
8. Snyder's nine revision questions (at the end of Section 11 of `sw-story-structure`)

For parallel review, each agent runs only one checklist, and the output format is uniform: `checklist number-question number | location (scene/page) | one sentence on the problem | one sentence of advice`.

## 5. Filled-In Example (Abridged, Fictional Project)

```markdown
# 《夜班》 (Night Shift) story bible

## Project Information
- Genre / Medium: feature film, 95 minutes, family drama
- Entry path: from scratch
- Reference film list: 《东京物语》 (Tokyo Story) (events off-screen), 《海边的曼彻斯特》 (Manchester by the Sea) (an ending without reconciliation), 《一一》 (Yi Yi) (echoes across its multiple storylines)

## Current Stage
- Stage: 3
- Next step: give the father a knife (he holds his daughter's tuition money)

## Decided Decisions
- Premise: an excessive sense of responsibility leads to estrangement between family members
- Controlling idea: family members lose each other because each one takes on the other's burden instead of speaking to the other (pessimistic)
- Ending: the daughter moves out; the father cooks for himself for the first time, burns the rice, and eats it all
- Protagonist and opponent: daughter vs. father; binding object = the house they share and the mother's illness

## 1 Premise and Theme
- Theme words: responsibility; the daughter's starting attitude, "to bear it is to love" → ending attitude, "only what is spoken is love"; the father's attitude from beginning to end is "to bear it is to love" (unchanged, which constitutes the tragedy)
- xìhé: each of the three family members keeps the same secret on behalf of the other two (the mother's illness)
- World rules: the mother's illness must be conveyed in one line by the doctor on page 8; after that, the film never explains it again

## 2 Structure
- Midpoint: the daughter believes her father has agreed to let her go away (false victory); all is lost: the father uses the tuition money to pay for the mother's surgery
- BS2: theme p5 / catalyst p11 / act two p24 / midpoint p48 / all is lost p66 / act three p74 / final image p95
- Tonal image: the burnt rice

## Decision Log
- 2026-09-06 Changed the ending from "father and daughter reconcile" to "the father finishes the burnt rice" ← the original ending was too neat and contradicted the pessimistic controlling idea
```

## 6. Opening Script for Resuming a Session (for the AI)

After reading the bible, say three sentences to the user:

> Project [X], [genre/length]. Last time we reached Stage N ([stage name]); decided: [one sentence on the premise], [one sentence on the ending]. The next step is [the "Next step" in the bible]. I will continue from there unless you want to change any of the above.

Do not restate the whole bible, and do not ask again about items already decided.
