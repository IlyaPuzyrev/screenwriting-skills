《唐人街》 (Chinatown), first ten pages: 寇莱 (Curly) cries over the photos of his wife's affair; 吉蒂斯 (Gittes) picks "a cheaper bottle from a row of rather expensive whiskey" for him — "If you want to kill somebody and get away with it, you've got to have money" (a prophecy of the ending); the frosted glass on the door, "Private Investigations," = the dramatic situation; on page five, the fake 墨尔雷太太 (Mrs. Mulwray), "I believe my husband is seeing another woman," = the dramatic premise; on page eight, City Hall, "Los Angeles is a city built on a desert," = the background; on page ten, the sheep farmer bursting in, "Who paid you to do this?" = the dramatic question posed again. The writer does not describe Gittes's appearance, only details (the white linen suit, the sweat wiped away).

## III. Beat-by-Beat BS2 Example: 《特工佳丽》 (Miss Congeniality)

Opening: as a child she punches a boy and is isolated → theme: "I don't need femininity" → catalyst: a murder at the beauty pageant → debate: "Can she do it?" (the mentor's makeover) → the miniskirt transformation and the fall flat on her face → fun and games (tackling a suspect while water glasses play music) → the B story is her relationship with the contestants → all is lost = her boss orders her out and she loses her FBI identity (whiff of death); the mentor leaves a dress → Act Three: the girls embrace her → on stage she performs self-defense with Benjamin (tough + sexy) → final image: surrounded by women, she wins "Miss Congeniality."

《记忆碎片》 (Memento): a gimmick, a box-office flop (Snyder's judgment, used as a counterexample to "high concept ≠ gimmick").

## IV. Nine-Beat Example: 《乔乔的异想世界》 (Jojo Rabbit)

- Basic exposition: ordered to kill the rabbit → refuses → "Jojo Rabbit" (the inciting incident).
- The argument: imaginary Hitler urges him to "be a rabbit" → throws a hand grenade and injures himself = "a long way to go" (the weak first commitment).
- Initial progress: sees hanged resistance fighters; discovers Elsa; interrogates → mocks → asks rational questions.
- Raising the stakes: argues with his mother, grows fonder of Elsa.
- Midpoint: butterflies in his stomach = love (the second commitment).
- Commitment: Gestapo search, Elsa poses as Inge, the captain helps verify.
- Crisis: mother hanged → attacks Elsa → rejects Hitler → opens his heart for the first time.
- Confrontation: city falls, the captain spits on him to save him.
- Resolution: tells Elsa he loves her, takes her outside to dance.

《冬天的骨头》 (Winter's Bone) in three acts: Act One — she discovers her father mortgaged the house, decides to find him, and no one will help; Act Two — she perseveres under threats, Teardrop helps, at the midpoint her father is absent from court / a one-week deadline, she storms Milton's place, is beaten, and Teardrop rescues her; Act Three — the woman takes her to the body, a chainsaw cuts off the hand, she hands it to the police, the house is saved, and Teardrop learns who the killer is and goes off to take revenge. Distinction: the rhythm of cause and effect — effort often goes unrewarded, turning points have no direct explanation, and the isolation deepens the resonance.

## V. McKee's Proportions and Design Points

- Scene count: 40–60 scenes, averaging 2.5 minutes; pair a one-minute scene with a four-minute scene; when racing toward an act climax, shorten scenes and increase speed in order to **earn the right to pause**; the major-reversal scene itself is long, slow, and tense ("the climax is not a brief explosion but a profound change").
- Act proportions: Act One ≈25% (climax at 20–30 minutes); the last act is the shortest, ≤20 minutes; the Act Two swamp is solved with subplots (a one-act A, a two-act B, a three-act C, five reversals in all) or by adding acts (a mid-act climax).
- Principle of transition: the third element between two scenes = something shared or opposed — a characterization trait (a mischievous child → a childish adult), an action (foreplay → afterglow), an object (greenhouse → jungle), a line of dialogue, the quality of light (dawn shadow → sunset glow), a sound (waves → snoring), an idea (blank canvas → dying old man).
- Progress: Act One tense → Act Two opens relaxed → then accelerates past the previous act, like a distance runner changing pace; "turning the screw slowly will exhaust the audience."
- Four layers of negative progress (building the wall of negativity): positive value → contrary value (unfairness) → contradictory value (injustice) → negation of the negation (tyranny / "the negative that looks positive"). Generally Act One reaches the contrary value, the following acts reach the contradictory value, and the last act reaches the negation of the negation.
- Four effects of a turning point: surprise → increased curiosity → insight → new direction; "why" sends the audience back through the earlier text, where the answer has been quietly planted. A turning scene has only two methods: **action or revelation**; strong revelations come from backstory held back until the crisis.
- Setup and payoff: a setup has one layer of meaning on first viewing and a second, third, and fourth on looking back (Cross's "for my daughter"); a payoff becomes a new setup; logic can be reconstructed backward.
- Diminishing emotional returns: three adjacent tragic scenes → tears → sobs → laughter; there are only two emotions (pleasure/pain), and tone cannot replace emotion.
- Variants of crisis placement: usually the last moment of the same scene (《末路狂花》 (Thelma & Louise) stretches it with slow motion and a freeze-frame); the crisis turns at the penultimate act and the entire last act is climactic action (《卡萨布兰卡》 (Casablanca): Rick gives Ilsa back to Laszlo → a 15-minute escape plan); very rarely the crisis follows immediately after the inciting incident and the whole film is one climactic action (Bond accepts the mission; 《离开拉斯维加斯》 (Leaving Las Vegas) decides to drink himself to death).

## VI. Lu Jun: A Library of Opening and Ending Examples

### Examples of the Eight Openings
| Method | Example |
|---|---|
| Conflict method | 《一个晴朗的早晨》 (One Fine Morning): a couple meet in a park, quarrel, then tell each other their romantic histories and discover they were once lovers; 《雨夜》 (Rainy Night): "Grandpa, I know who you're waiting for, but I'm telling you, the current township head won't come!" |
| Situation-planting method | 《另一条出路》 (Another Way Out): they come straight to the point, complaining that they are treated as a couple, and decide each will have one affair; 《开心歌》 (Joyful Song): the offstage chorus before the curtain, "Today is New Year's Eve and the red honor roll is posted — let's see who is the richest man," plus 李笑笑 (Li Xiaoxiao): "Let's put on a show in which the God of Wealth laments his poverty and then we'll celebrate the New Year." |
| Heightening method | 《仁爱之心》 (A Heart of Benevolence): trivial conversation while lives hang in the balance; two tourists bet on the age of a cliff-jumper, "ten to a hundred"; 《定心丸》 (The Reassurance Pill): the benefits of the fish load are heightened — "even if the emperor himself wanted to swap, I'd have to think about it." |
| Surprise method | 《蚕乡女》 (The Silkworm Village Girl): the star performer hands in a transfer request just as a blind girl arrives to audition. |
| Opposite-course method | 《瓜熟时节》 (When the Melons Ripen): 阿福 (A Fu), who "eats the big households," says, "If you'd told me, I'd have come to get it myself." |
| Blunt-blow method | 《种花人家》 (The Flower-Growing Family): on the day the cultural room opens, the father rules that three flower baskets must be woven every night. |
| Self-introduction method | Brecht's 《例外与常规》 (The Exception and the Rule): chorus and the merchant's confession; 《车》 (The Car): the host — "In thirty-two minutes, ninety-seven people went around this car." |
| Driven-to-rebellion method | 《骑墙记》 (Sitting on the Fence): 阿毛 (A Mao) secretly plants breeding rabbits and sits on the wall while 蓝花 (Lan Hua) interrogates the "thief." |

### Examples of the Eight Endings
| Method | Example |
|---|---|
| Thunderclap | 《十二磅钱的神情》 (The Look of the Twelve Pounds): the lady — "Are they very expensive?" "What?" "Those typewriters."; 《权充监察员》 (Acting Inspector): next door — "I am the inspector; I've already lived next door to you for three nights."; 《坦白》 (Confession): "He would rather confess," Baldwin collapses into the chair — "confess"; 《卖杨梅》 (Selling Waxberries): "the engagement wine we drank last month" "Oh!..." the towel falls. |
| Unresolved | 《马戏团员》 (The Circus Performer): "Go? Who said so? No, no, no such thing!... I can't go," left alone on stage as the music drifts in; 《要不要原谅他》 (Should I Forgive Him?): the false leg, "Should I forgive him?" to the audience. |
| Extension | 《压迫》 (Oppression): "Ah, what is your name?" "I— ah— I—"; 《第七棵是玉兰树》 (The Seventh Tree Is a Magnolia): the benefactor will not meet, flowers are offered, the chorus sings. |
| Rebound | 《男高音歌手》 (The Tenor): after Helen shoots herself, "I still have one minute and ten seconds... I must catch this train so I can sing in Brussels tomorrow night"; 《见证》 (The Witness): a gunshot offstage (in the manner of O. Henry's 《警察与赞美诗》 ("The Cop and the Anthem")). |
| Finishing touch | 《钉上十字架的人》 (The Man on the Cross): Magdalene — "The real fault is in himself... he probably still thinks that if he died we would get something out of it"; 《称婆婆》 (Weighing Mother-in-Law): "Everyone has a scale in their heart." |
| Echo | 《打铜锣》 (Beating the Gong): Cai Jiu's three gongs and four lines before and after — "In harvest season the grain is gold; every household, watch your chickens and ducks" — first light, then heavy. |
| Cyclical | 《莫里生案件》 (The Morrison Case): "You have made me a positive citizen." "That's exactly where you're wrong; I wasn't before." "Beware one day of the anger of the masses"; 《主角登场》 (Enter the Leading Lady): Annie locks up the cards and writes another apology letter — "Why do you put out the light of the stars?" |
| Conclusion | 《三块钱国币》 (Three Yuan in National Currency): "A draw"; 《葛麻》 (Ge Ma): "Pay my respects to the Zhang family of Dahong"; 《摇篮曲》 (Lullaby), 《赶不走的媳妇》 (The Daughter-in-Law Who Won't Leave), 《双推磨》 (Two People Turning the Mill): closing chorus. |

### Examples of the Three Methods of Transformation
- The power of environment: 《款待客人的法律》 (The Law of Hospitality): three greedy, snobbish customs clerks cannot stop Peter; London workers strike in support; the higher-ups give way and let everyone through.
- The power of setup: 《月亮上升的时候》 (When the Moon Rises): the folk song in three steps — love song → song of oppression → contact code; the inspector's monologue: "If I hadn't joined the police then, I might be the one escaping from prison and hiding in a dark corner right now..."
- The power of action: 《阿二接妻》 (A'er Brings His Wife Home): the basket-weaving duet — "The basket relies on the bamboo warp, just as Axiu holds the family together... husband and wife weave the treasure basin together"; 《赶不走的媳妇》 (The Daughter-in-Law Who Won't Leave): the three answers (accusing the husband to save a relative / aborting to hold the family together / selling her dowry to repay the debt and pay for medical treatment) tear the paper — the content of the narration has already been shown in the first half, so it is also the power of action.

### Examples of Climaxes
- Driven by character: 《妇女代表》 (The Women's Representative): Zhang Guirong goes from forbearance to tearing the whip to taking the land deed — "Of the two land deeds one is mine; of the three rooms one end is mine... I am a person, I am a person of the nation, not a beast you feed! Don't forget, the days when women were not treated as human beings are gone forever" — she picks up the child and leaves.
- Driven by environment: 《赵小兰》 (Zhao Xiaolan): the village chairman's introduction letter → the Wei family's son breaks off the engagement → the elder sister: "Father, I'm divorced, why shouldn't I be happy?" → the mother straightens her back; 《警察与小偷》 (The Policeman and the Thief): the fake policeman, under the patrolman's guidance, enters the role, helps a blind woman, asks passersby the way, "catches one" — the other is himself — and hands over the stolen wallet.
- Accident: 《石祠堂》 (The Stone Ancestral Hall): the mother saves money to build an ancestral hall for her son who "died gloriously"; the son suddenly returns for money; the mother calls the patrol — "This man is a thief and a murderer, he has no passport"; the son is led away — "Woman! Pity your son!"; the mother falls dead before the icon — "What is one person compared with an ideal?"
- Example of the obligatory scene: 《袜子的风采》 (The Splendor of Socks): burn fifty thousand pairs of socks → Robert signs a $12 million order → the uncle: "I only burned a few boxes; the rest were in old newspapers" → "Two options: burn them or leave your post" → "Three rules: I have the right to operate, the right to develop, the right to control personnel" → "I am not your uncle!" → the model troupe's question-and-answer song: "Am I too cruel?" "It's not cruelty; it's because you love Sasha too deeply."

## VII. Hicks's Three-Act Outline of 《雨人》 (Rain Man): Attraction / Anticipation / Satisfaction

- Act One: Charlie learns that his father's $3 million estate has gone to a trustee he does not know; he finds the institution and discovers an autistic older brother, Raymond — attraction: a selfish man discovers he has been disinherited and has a brother.
- Act Two: Charlie "kidnaps" Raymond and crosses America to force a share of the inheritance; the audience expects him to use Raymond (counting cards in Las Vegas) and also expects him to change; each stop defeats an expectation and creates a new one.
- Act Three: at the hearing Charlie gives up guardianship — satisfaction: what he gains is not the money but his brother; the expectation is paid off, but not in the way the audience imagined.
- Hicks's "minimum action" exercise: Stanley and the dog — use the fewest actions to let the audience know who a person is; the "eight-question surprise test": ask eight questions about each character; only if the answers can surprise you does the character stand up.

## VIII. Index of Structural Failure Cases

- Set-up too long: 《少数派报告》 (Minority Report) waits 40 minutes to drop the hook; 《遇见波莉》 (Along Came Polly) lays out the wedding, the honeymoon, and the affair in full.
- Soft belly in Act Two: repeating the scale of Act One action (McKee); "the Cat in the Hat runs around and nothing happens" (Snyder's "turns, turns, turns").
- Manufactured climax: at a Shanghai festival, five short plays all knelt (Lu Jun).
- Weak transformation: relying on the municipal party secretary's phone call (《铃响后》 (After the Bell)), a Soviet film (《家务事》 (Family Affairs)), fake poisoning (an old trap).
- Ending too neat: laid-off workers become advanced cooks and everyone is happy; ending too drawn out: green mushrooms like an environmental exhibition; ending too jumpy: bird fighting paired with the 《黄河大合唱》 (Yellow River Cantata).
- Coincidental turn ending: a hurricane, an elephant herd, a car crash, a T. rex (McKee's "god from the machine").
- Insufficient antagonist: if the protagonist is Superman, he must become an "underdog" (two nuclear bombs, choose the lesser of two evils → Lois dies → reverse the earth's rotation).
- Double mumbo jumbo: 《蜘蛛侠》 (Spider-Man): radioactive spider + biochemical accident; 《天兆》 (Signs): aliens + crisis of faith (Snyder).
- Watch out for the glacier: the danger approaches too slowly and poses no threat (the volcano does not show the townspeople until page 75).
